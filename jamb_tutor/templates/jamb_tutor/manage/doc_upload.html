Good to know

  • PDF and .docx only, up to 4 MB. Old .doc files must be saved as .docx first.
  • Scanned PDFs work because Gemini reads the page images.
  • The file is processed a couple of pages at a time, so keep this page open until it finishes.
  • Check the answers. If the document has no answer key, Gemini works the answer out itself and the question is flagged so you can verify it.
  • Questions that need a diagram are flagged and left unticked.
  • A question that runs across a page break may come out incomplete. It will be flagged for you to fix.
